Research and validation notes

Evaluation is separate from shopping. We report product fact accuracy, budget and spatial feasibility, and user satisfaction independently. HK$3,000 / 8,000 / 15,000 are preliminary test conditions, not validated market tiers.

Housing and household information acts as supporting context and does not prescribe brands. A participant’s real budget is recorded separately from the assigned experiment condition. Fair comparison requires the same frozen catalogue and task.

Human-reviewed ground truth, formal comparative testing and satisfaction validation are still pending. A room shape estimated from area is an assumption, not proof of real spatial compliance.
